## Problem

Economics research at Université de Neuchâtel needed both rigorous classical time-series methods and modern deep-learning forecasting, applied to real econometric questions, not toy datasets.

## Approach

- Statistical analysis and modeling for economics research, including deep-learning-based time-series forecasting.
- Supported ETL processes for the underlying research data and integrated GenAI tooling into the research workflow.
- Worked with a public Swiss electricity-market dataset (energy-charts.info): stationarity testing (Augmented Dickey-Fuller), outlier detection, feature engineering, classical and deep-learning forecasting models.

## Outcome

Forecasting models and statistical analysis used directly in academic research output, spanning both classical (ARIMA/STATA) and deep-learning (TensorFlow/Keras) approaches.
